## Adopt as independently written operational policy

- Honor the requested deliverable before applying a default response packet.
- Give each artifact one primary operation; split genuinely sequential
  operations instead of mixing incompatible task contracts.
- Bind each active reference to a source, target, active scope, preserved
  traits, and excluded inheritance.
- Distinguish a complete visible-person reference from an explicitly narrowed
  face-only or identity-only reference.
- Define which dimensions an example contributes rather than inheriting its
  story, point of view, camera, style, dialogue, or outcome wholesale.
- Normalize video extension to an unambiguous interval before or after the
  source.
- Audit action, performance, camera, and sound as causal state changes and
  resolve contradictions before delivery.

These are project-authored practice rules. They are not represented as a
Dreamina, Seedance, ModelArk, LAS, or Higgsfield product guarantee.
